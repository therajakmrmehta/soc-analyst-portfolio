## Introduction to Phishing

  A type of cyberattack in which attackers use fake emails, messages, or websites to trick people into revealing sensitive information, such as passwords, bank details, or login credentials.

    Example: An attacker sends a fake bank email asking the user to click a link and verify their account password.

## Phases of the Intrusion Kill Chain

- Reconnaissance:

      The attacker gathers information about the target.
- Weaponization:

      The attacker prepares a malicious payload.
- Delivery:

      The attacker sends the payload through email, websites, or other methods.
- Exploitation:

      The attacker exploits a vulnerability to gain access.
- Installation:

      Malware is installed on the target system.
- Command and Control (C2):

      The compromised system communicates with the attacker's server.
- Actions on Objectives:

      The attacker achieves their goal, such as stealing data or disrupting systems.

## Spoofing

   A cyberattack in which an attacker disguises their identity or impersonates a trusted source to trick a victim.

    Example: An attacker sends an email using a forged sender address that appears to belong to a legitimate company.
## What is an Email Header?
The header is a section of the email containing information such as sender, recipient, and date. There are also components such as 'Return-Path', 'Reply-To', and 'Received'. Below you can see the header details of an example email.

- Return-Path:

      Shows the email address where bounce messages or delivery failure notifications are sent.
- DomainKeys and DKIM Signatures:

      DKIM helps verify that an email was authorized by the sending domain and that signed parts of the message were not altered.
- Message-ID:

      A unique identifier assigned to an email message.
- MIME-Version:

      Indicates the MIME format version used to support email content such as attachments, HTML, and images.
- X-Spam-Status:

      A header that may show whether an email was flagged as spam by a mail filtering system.
